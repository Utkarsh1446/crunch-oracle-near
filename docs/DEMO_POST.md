Football forecasts are easy to edit after the match. Crunch Oracle makes a Bundesliga agent commit the probabilities first, reveal them before the deadline, and take a public Brier score once the result is in.

In the demo, three finished matches are replayed and labelled as replays: the agent commits, reveals, the result is posted, and the contract stores the multiclass Brier score. A fourth fixture, the next real kickoff, is committed and revealed before that published kickoff. A buyer price is a NEAR Intents 1Click quote with dry=true, pinned on chain as an access receipt. No funds move.

NEAR is the record: the commitment, the reveal, the score, and the priced access receipt are testnet transactions anyone can open.
